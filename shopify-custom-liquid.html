How these prices are calculated

Market research data, not financial advice.

Median, not average

Each card's price is the median of every current eBay listing for it. A median ignores one seller pricing at $2 and another at $2,000, so the number tracks what the card really trades at.

Refresh cadence

Listings are re-pulled roughly every four hours. Daily medians are stored, and the 24h / 7d / 30d columns compare today's median against the stored snapshot for that lookback.

Condition and grading

Prices reflect raw, near-mint singles. Graded slabs, sealed lots and bundle listings are filtered out before the median is taken, so a PSA 10 will sit well above the number shown here.

Coverage gaps

A card needs at least three live listings to get a price. Anything thinner is held back rather than published, which is why very new promos can take a few days to appear.
